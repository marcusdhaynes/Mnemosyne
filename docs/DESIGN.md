# Mnemosyne Design System — 'Editorial Archive'

- **Art direction:** A contemporary photographic atelier: gallery-like calm, understated luxe, quiet confidence, no gaudy glass effects or overanimated gimmicks.
- **Color:** Ink Charcoal `#17191B`, soft chalk `#F8F7F4`, antique brass `#AA8D68`, aged sage `#363C3C`, ivory `#E3D5BC`.
- **Typography:** Editorial Georgian serif headlines (fallback-safe) + DM Sans/system body; restrained all-caps micro-labels; generous leading, precise spacing.
- **Composition:** 262 px navigation rail, warm neutral workspace, 77 px utility header, feature-story hero, editorial statistics, non-cropped contained full-viewer originals, expandable masonry-like grid.
- **Interaction:** 150–380 ms restrained transitions, keyboard navigation, accessible labels and focus indicators, reduced-motion support, two-column narrow-screen gallery.
- **UX constraints:** no fabricated real-user photographs; illustrated cards are decorative placeholders. Never silently delete, relocate or upload originals. Import and organization progress must be observable and cancellable in production.
- **Design QA:** examine at 320, 390, 768, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px; test keyboard-only and screen reader navigation; target WCAG AA in future audit; app shell first content < 1s on realistic devices after profiling.
